About the University Timetable Management System

The University Timetable Management System provides a centralized platform for organizing, managing, publishing, and accessing teaching and examination timetables.

Vision and Mission

To simplify academic scheduling through technology and provide reliable access to approved timetables.

System Features

  • Centralized timetable management.
  • Automated timetable generation.
  • Conflict detection and resolution.
  • Teaching and examination timetable separation.
  • Published timetable access.
  • PDF and Excel downloads.
  • Responsive access across devices.
  • Secure role-based administration.

How the System Works

Administrators generate and publish timetables; students and staff browse approved schedules by programme, year and semester.
